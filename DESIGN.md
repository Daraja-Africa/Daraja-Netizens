# Daraja Netizens visual system

## North star

Daraja Netizens is a secure operations console that feels calm, warm, and dependable. The interface uses editorial restraint to make attendance, check-in, and live-session state easy to scan.

## Visual world

- **Palette:** warm linen background (`#faf5ee`), white paper surfaces, espresso ink (`#2a2420`), burnt sienna primary (`#c2652a`), dusty rose for secondary emphasis, and emerald for verified state.
- **Typography:** EB Garamond for product headings and high-value metrics; Manrope for labels, controls, and body copy.
- **Surfaces:** thin warm borders, low-contrast paper shadows, generous spacing, and restrained radial washes rather than cold gradients or glass panels.
- **Controls:** terracotta primary actions, warm outlined/quiet secondary controls, rounded corners between 10–18px, and visible focus rings.

## Composition

The desktop shell uses a persistent workspace rail and an open content panel. On narrow screens the rail becomes a sticky mobile console header with compact segmented navigation. Operational views lead with current state, then metrics, then the action or ledger needed to complete the task.

## Interaction

Secure session status remains visible. Empty, loading, camera fallback, and API error states use direct copy. The app preserves keyboard focus, native form semantics, and the existing module/view navigation model.
